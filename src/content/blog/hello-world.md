I have made a website. This is it.

It exists partly because I have accumulated a small number of projects that are easier to point to than to explain, and partly because I have started writing things down more — notes, observations, the occasional longer reflection — and having somewhere to put them seems reasonable.

The site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and Tailwind CSS, which I chose mostly because the setup time was short and the result is fast. A large part of it was assembled with AI assistance, which feels appropriate for a site that is partly *about* using AI tools in everyday work.

I am not sure how often I will post here. Probably when something is worth writing about. That is the plan, anyway.
